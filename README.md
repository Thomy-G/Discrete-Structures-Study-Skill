# Bar-Ilan University — Discrete Structures (89-2197)
## Interactive Exam & Quiz Practice Simulator

This repository contains an agentic study skill for **Antigravity IDE** designed specifically for Bar-Ilan University's **Discrete Structures** course taught by **Dr. Arnold Filtser**.

---

## 🌟 Architecture & Features

- **3 Quiz Tiers mapped to 13 Homeworks**:
  1. **Quiz 1 (HW 01 – HW 04):** Relations, Posets, Lexicographic Orders, Graph Foundations, Trees, Bipartite Graphs, Cardinality ($\aleph_0, \aleph$), Cantor Diagonal, CSB.
  2. **Quiz 2 (HW 05 – HW 08):** Euler & Hamilton Circuits, Matchings, Hall's Marriage Theorem, Planar Graphs ($V-E+F=2$), Graph Coloring & Brooks' Theorem.
  3. **Quiz 3 (HW 09 – HW 13):** Recurrence Relations, Generating Functions, Ramsey Theory, Turán's Theorem, Probabilistic Method, Prüfer Codes, Gale-Shapley.
- **Strict Grader Evaluation Mode:**
  - Evaluates proofs against Bar-Ilan mathematical rigor standards.
  - Automatically appends HTML score badges and a final grade tally into your answer file.
- **LaTeX-Suite Pre-configured:**
  - Equipped with `obsidian-latex-suite` snippets for rapid mathematical typesetting in Obsidian.

---

## 🚀 How to Practice

In your Antigravity chat, trigger a practice session using any of the following prompts:

- **Midterm Quizzes (בחני אמצע):**
  - *"Start a full simulation for Quiz 1"* (or *"סמלץ לי את בוחן 1"*)
  - *"Drill me on HW 03 and HW 04"* (or *"תרגל אותי על תרגילי בית 3 ו-4 לקראת הבוחן"*)
- **Comprehensive Final Exams (מבחני גמר סמסטריאליים מועד א'/ב'):**
  - *"Start a full final exam simulation"* (or *"תן לי סימולציה של מבחן סופי מלא מועד א'"*)
  - Generates a full 3-hour, 5-question multi-part theoretical exam covering the entire 13-week syllabus.
- **Topic Drills:**
  - *"Drill me on Cardinality and Cantor-Schröder-Bernstein"*
  - *"Drill me on Matchings and Hall's Marriage Theorem"*
  - *"Drill me on Ramsey Theory and the Probabilistic Method"*

### The Workflow:
1. The assistant creates a dedicated subfolder in `Practice_Sessions/`.
2. It writes `Questions.md` and a blank `Answers_Template.md`.
3. You open `Answers_Template.md` in Obsidian and type your solutions.
4. Type in chat: **"Review this"** (or **"בדוק את התשובות שלי"**).
5. The assistant reviews your submission, annotates your answer file with feedback badges, and unlocks `Solutions.md`.
